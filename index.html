RemoteShell

Your servers. Anywhere.

RemoteShell gives you secure access to your servers from your iPhone and iPad. Manage SSH sessions, browse files with SFTP, and work with remote systems wherever you are. Keep credentials secure with Apple's Keychain and protect the app with Face ID.

  • Full SSH terminal with multiple sessions, ANSI colors and hardware keyboard support
  • Password and SSH key authentication (Ed25519, ECDSA, RSA) with host key verification
  • SFTP file manager with uploads, downloads and transfer progress
  • RDP and VNC remote desktop, optionally tunneled through SSH
  • Two-factor authentication, jump hosts, port forwarding and persistent tmux sessions
  • No account, no cloud, no tracking: your credentials stay on your device
