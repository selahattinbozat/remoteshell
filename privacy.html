Privacy Policy

RemoteShell for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 4, 2026

Summary

RemoteShell does not require an account, does not run any servers that receive your data, and does not collect personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Your credentials

Passwords, SSH private keys and key passphrases are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device, with the "this device only" protection class. They are not synced to iCloud, are not included in device-to-device migrations, and are never uploaded anywhere. They are never written to logs.

Server profiles and app data

Server names, hostnames, usernames, notes, tags, saved commands, imported key metadata and trusted host keys are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Direct connections

When you connect, RemoteShell connects directly from your device to the server you configured (SSH, SFTP or VNC). Commands you type, terminal output and the files you transfer travel only between your device and that server. The developer never sees them.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

RemoteShell contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party AI services, and does not collect usage data. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, email address or payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you enable the app lock, authentication is performed by iOS. RemoteShell never receives or stores biometric data.

Local network

iOS may ask for permission to access your local network. It is only used to connect to servers on your local network that you configure yourself.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide anonymized crash reports to the developer. These do not contain your credentials or server data.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in RemoteShell → Settings → Erase All Data, or by deleting the app. Erase All Data removes all server profiles, keys, trusted hosts, saved commands, downloaded files and every Keychain item created by the app.

Children

RemoteShell is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
